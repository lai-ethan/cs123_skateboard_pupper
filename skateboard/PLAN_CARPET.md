# Carpet plan: make the split ride roll on carpet (steer v9)

Written 2026-11-24. First hardware videos are in; nothing here has been tried
on the robot yet.

**Goal:** a split steer policy that rolls the board on carpet as well as on a
hard floor, without sliding along the deck.

**Scope:** `Mjlab-SkateSplit-Ride-Pupper-v3` and
`Mjlab-SkateSplit-Steer-Pupper-v3` (steer builds on ride). Balance tasks and
the centred tasks keep their behaviour.

## What the hardware run showed (2026-11-24, Gates carpet)

- Split balance and centred balance held for 17 to 18 s.
- Split ride (steer v6): the floor legs stroke, the board does not roll.
- In one clip the robot creeps from mid-deck to the nose over about 10 s
  while the board stays put.

## What the sim says

Steer v6, 0.3 m/s, 16 boards, 10 s, wheel drag pinned (nominal drag is about
0.28 N of rolling resistance in total):

| Wheel drag | Speed (share of asked) | Resets | Main reset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| x3 (top of the trained range) | 0.94 | 0 | - |
| x6 | 0.70 | 4 | `board_slid`, `board_drifted` |
| x10 | 0.36 | 12 | `board_slid` |
| x15 | 0.18 | 25 | `board_slid` |
| x25 | 0.10 | 41 | `board_slid` |

High drag reproduces both hardware symptoms: the board stalls and the feet
slide along the deck. So carpet is taken to be about x15 to x25 of nominal
(4 to 7 N). This is not measured on the real board.

The TA's advice (2026-11-24): randomize board height, friction between the
torso and the board, and foot friction on carpet (Gates carpet differs from
other carpets).

## Task 1: carpet randomization for the split ride and steer

Files: `src/mjlab/tasks/pupper_skate/mdp/events.py`,
`src/mjlab/tasks/pupper_skate/pupper_skate_split_env_cfg.py`,
`skateboard/tools/ride_audit.py`, `tests/test_pupper_skate_split.py`,
`tests/test_skate_ride_audit.py`, `skateboard/SIM2REAL.md`.

All four changes are in the split ride config (the function that today sets
`wheel_drag`, `deck_foot_friction` and `floor_foot_friction`), so steer
inherits them. Each new number is a named module constant with a comment
giving the evidence above.

1. **Wheel drag up to carpet.**
   - `scale_joint_drag` gets a keyword `log_uniform: bool = False`. When
     true the factor is drawn log-uniformly over `scale_range` (uniform in
     the log of the factor); false keeps today's draw exactly.
   - Split ride: `WHEEL_DRAG_SCALE_RANGE = (0.5, 25.0)`, `log_uniform=True`.
     Log-uniform so half the boards are still under about x3.5 (hard floor)
     and the policy keeps its hard-floor ride.
2. **Floor grip up to carpet.** `FLOOR_FOOT_FRICTION_RANGE = (0.5, 1.6)`
   (today 0.5 to 1.2). Deck feet stay `(0.5, 1.4)`, as a named constant
   `DECK_FOOT_FRICTION_RANGE`.
3. **Torso and knees on the deck.** Today the split ride drops the
   `deck_friction` event, so every non-foot contact with the deck has
   friction 1.0. Keep the event in the split ride with
   `DECK_BODY_FRICTION_RANGE = (0.3, 1.2)`. Feet keep their own draw (geom
   priority 1), so this reaches only body, knee and shin contacts. Update the
   comment that explains why the event was dropped.
4. **Deck height.** Split ride and steer use
   `SPLIT_RIDE_DECK_HEIGHT_RANGE = (-0.035, 0.015)`: deck top 6.8 to 11.8 cm
   (today the top is 11.3). Balance keeps `DECK_HEIGHT_RANGE`. Before
   settling the top value, check with the existing split stance tools or
   tests that the split stance still holds at +1.5 cm; if it does not, keep
   +0.01 and say so in the report with the evidence.
5. **Ride audit rows.** Add `wheel_drag_x6`, `wheel_drag_x10`,
   `wheel_drag_x15` and `wheel_drag_x25` (500 steps each, like x2 and x3),
   and `floor_grip_1.6` following the existing `floor_grip_0.4` row.
6. **Tests, written first and seen to fail:**
   - `scale_joint_drag` with `log_uniform=True` over (0.5, 25): every factor
     is inside the range, the median over 4096 envs is within 15% of
     `sqrt(0.5 * 25)`, all four wheels of one env share one factor, damping
     and frictionloss scale by the same factor. `log_uniform=False`
     reproduces today's values for a fixed seed.
   - The split ride and steer configs carry the four ranges above; the split
     balance config still has today's `deck_height` range and no
     `wheel_drag`; the centred ride is unchanged.
   - The existing test that asserts the split ride has no `deck_friction`
     event is updated to the new range, not deleted.
   - The audit has the five new rows and they configure the env as named.
7. **Docs.** `skateboard/SIM2REAL.md`: a short "Carpet" section with the
   table above, the new ranges, and that the carpet drag is a sim estimate,
   not a measurement.

## Task 2: train steer v9 (controller, Colab)

Push to the team repo, then resume from steer v6 (`hfd3qhwr`,
`model_26998.pt`) for 3000 iterations, 4096 envs, on a GPU runtime.

## Task 3: evaluate and decide (controller)

- Ride audit with all rows, gait report at stick 0, ±0.15, ±0.3, ±0.5 on
  seed 0, `timed_ride.py` at stick 0 at drag x1 and x15.
- Pass bar, all of:
  - every audit row v6 passes still passes;
  - `wheel_drag_x15`: speed at least 0.6 of asked, no more than 2 resets in
    16 boards;
  - `wheel_drag_x25`: speed at least 0.4 of asked;
  - gait report passes at stick 0 and ±0.15.
- If it passes: export `split_steer_v9_*` with the 0.3 m/s clip, add it to
  `skateboard/policies/README.md`, and update the hardware checklist. If it
  does not: v6 stays, and the report says which bar it missed.

## Accounting

- Skipped: reward changes; a board-speed observation (would break the
  resume from v6).
- Assumed: carpet is x15 to x25 of the nominal wheel drag; the torso does
  not normally rest on the deck in the split stance.
- Issues: at the lowest deck grip (0.5) the feet may not be able to hold a
  7 N push, so the policy may not reach full speed on carpet without grip
  tape on the deck.
