# Heading plan: make the ride hold its line (steer v13)

Written 2026-11-26. Sim only until a policy passes; nothing here runs on the
robot.

**Goal:** with the stick centred, the board keeps the heading it started on,
so a 25 s ride ends near its line instead of tens of cm to one side.

**Scope:** `Mjlab-SkateSplit-Steer-Pupper-v3` rewards and the sim tools.
Balance, the split ride task and the centred tasks are untouched. The
robot-side heading hold and the exported file's `heading_hold` block do not
change.

Naming: v11 was the extra carpet run, and a teammate's `board_progress` run
is called v12, so this one is v13.

## What the investigation found (steer v10, 16 boards, 0.3 m/s, x3 drag, 25 s)

How the hold works (same formula in sim and on the robot):

- With the stick under 0.1 and the board moving, the yaw command the policy
  sees is `clip(kp * (target - robot heading), ±clip)` with kp 1.0 and clip
  0.1 (`src/mjlab/tasks/velocity/mdp/velocity_command.py:234-248`). The sim
  captures the board's heading as the target
  (`src/mjlab/tasks/pupper_skate/mdp/commands.py:32`); the robot captures its
  own IMU yaw. There is no deadband.
- A heading error of 3 / 5 / 7 degrees gives a command of 0.052 / 0.087 / 0.1.

Why the heading settles several degrees off:

- The policy turns the board only about 0.185 rad/s per unit of that command
  (0.06 to 0.34 across boards), so the hold has at most about 0.019 rad/s of
  authority.
- With a zero command the policy still turns the board by itself: -0.032 to
  +0.019 rad/s across boards (mean -0.008, mean size 0.015). The heading
  settles where the weak correction balances that pull.
- The robot also stands rotated about 1 degree against the board (the same
  way in 15 of 16 boards), which the hold cannot see.
- Sideways drift is explained by board heading alone (predicted per board to
  within 0.8 cm).
- Why the policy follows corrections weakly: ignoring a 0.05 correction costs
  only 5% of `track_yaw_rate`, and `board_yaw_rate` and `board_alignment` are
  flat at 1 to 2 degrees.

Retuning the hold alone (no retraining), drift after 25 s:

| Hold | Range (cm) | Mean size (cm) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kp 1, clip 0.1 (shipped) | -56 to +85 | 29.0 |
| kp 4, clip 0.1 | -53 to +65 | 22.9 |
| kp 4, clip 0.3 | -51 to +34 | 20.4 |
| kp 4, ki 1, clip 0.1 | -72 to +67 | 24.8 |
| kp 4, ki 1, clip 0.3 | -41 to +19 | 14.3 |

The best retune halves the drift but needs a robot-side code change, pushes
the command above 0.1 for half the ride (which the policy reads as a carve),
and depends on IMU yaw drift that is not measured. So this plan trains the
policy to answer the existing hold instead, and leaves the hold alone.

Not determined: the pull and IMU drift on hardware; why the pull is biased to
the right; anything beyond one seed, x3 drag and 0.3 m/s.

## Design

Two things make the hold work with its shipped gain and clip:

1. **The board should not turn by itself, and should turn when the hold asks.**
   Outside carves, pay the board's yaw rate for matching
   `HOLD_BOARD_GAIN * (yaw command)`. At zero command that target is zero
   (no pull); at the hold's largest command, 0.1, it is 0.03 rad/s, which the
   board can do (a medium carve already asks 0.03). With that response the
   loop's authority is 0.03 rad/s against today's 0.019, and the pull it has
   to beat is gone.
2. **The robot should stand square on the board.** Tighten the yaw part of
   `board_alignment`.

An earlier run (v8) paid for zero board yaw rate outside carves together with
a sideways-creep charge and failed its bar; the stick-0 pull did go away.
This plan uses the yaw-rate term without the creep charge, and scales its
target with the hold's command.

## Task 1: steer rewards that answer the hold, and a drift readout

Files: `src/mjlab/tasks/pupper/mdp/rewards.py` or wherever
`track_board_yaw_rate` and `carve_yaw_target` live,
`src/mjlab/tasks/pupper_skate/pupper_skate_split_env_cfg.py`,
`skateboard/tools/timed_ride.py`, `skateboard/tools/make_notebook.py` and the
regenerated notebook only if the reward table or its text needs it,
`tests/test_pupper_skate_split.py`, `tests/test_skate_timed_ride.py`,
`tests/test_skate_notebook.py` if touched.

1. `track_board_yaw_rate` gets a parameter `straight_gain: float = 0.0`,
   used only when `straight_min_speed` is set. Outside carves, in envs whose
   forward speed command is at least `straight_min_speed`, the board yaw-rate
   target is `straight_gain * (the yaw command the policy sees)`, with the
   same `std` as the carve target. `straight_gain = 0.0` is the v8 behaviour
   (target zero); `straight_min_speed = None` is today's behaviour exactly.
   Standing envs still get 0.
2. Steer config only:
   - `track_board_yaw_rate`: `straight_min_speed = STEER_YAW_MIN_SPEED`,
     `straight_gain = HOLD_BOARD_GAIN`, with a new constant
     `HOLD_BOARD_GAIN = 0.3`. Weight and std unchanged.
   - `board_alignment`: the yaw std from 0.1 to a new constant
     `STEER_BOARD_ALIGN_STD_YAW = 0.04` (the split ride keeps 0.1).
   - `board_side_creep` stays at weight 0.0 and `deck_feet_slide` at -2.0.
   - A comment at each constant with the numbers above that justify it (the
     0.185 response, the ±0.015 pull, the 1 degree rotation), and the steer
     docstring's list of what is paid updated.
3. `timed_ride.py`: add `side_m_abs_mean` (the mean over envs of the size of
   the sideways displacement at the end of the ride) next to
   `side_m_abs_max`. Nothing else in its output changes.
4. Tests first, seen to fail:
   - `track_board_yaw_rate` with `straight_min_speed` set and
     `straight_gain = 0.3`: a straight env at speed with yaw command 0.1 and
     board yaw rate 0.03 gets 1.0; with board yaw rate 0 it gets
     `exp(-(0.03 / std) ** 2)` to 3 decimals; with yaw command 0 and board yaw
     rate 0 it gets 1.0; a standing env gets 0; a carve env gets exactly what
     it gets today; `straight_gain = 0.0` reproduces the target-zero values;
     `straight_min_speed = None` reproduces today's values.
   - The steer config carries `straight_min_speed`, `straight_gain = 0.3` and
     the alignment yaw std 0.04; the split ride config has neither change;
     the steer's other weights are what they are today.
   - `side_m_abs_mean` on two synthetic boards ending 0.1 m left and 0.3 m
     right is 0.2.
5. If the notebook's reward table or text describes these terms, update the
   tool and regenerate; otherwise leave the notebook alone and say so.

## Task 3: evaluate and decide (controller)

- 25 s ride: `timed_ride.py` at 0.3 m/s, stick 0, x3 drag, 16 boards,
  `--ride-s 25`. v10 for comparison ends -56 to +85 cm, mean size 29.0 cm.
- Ride audit, all 21 rows.
- Gait report at stick 0, ±0.15, ±0.3, ±0.5 on seeds 0, 1, 2.
- Pass bar, all of:
  - 25 s drift: mean size at most 15 cm and no board past 40 cm;
  - every audit row v10 passes still passes, and `wheel_drag_x6` holds at
    least 0.9 of the asked speed;
  - gait report: no more than 1 failing run in 21, and at stick ±0.3 and
    ±0.5 the board turns 0.5 to 1.5 of what is asked on every seed.
- If it passes: export `split_steer_v13_*` with the 0.3 m/s clip as a
  provisional file after v6 and v10, with its audit, gait and drift numbers.
  If it does not: no export, and the report says which bar it missed.
